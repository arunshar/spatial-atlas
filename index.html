Conversation prompts

Speaker follow-ups

Lead with one precise connection, ask one technical question, then offer the poster or paper link. Do not turn the first interaction into a full project pitch.

Chuan Li

What is the minimum journal record needed to prove which observations and computed facts an answer consumed?

Trevor Darrell or Vincent Vanhoucke

When masks, depth, or point maps are uncertain, what condition should force a spatial agent to refuse rather than compute?

Rose Yu or Mengdi Wang

What evidence state would make an agent's scientific conclusion reproducible rather than merely plausible?

Philip Rathle

How should a graph distinguish observed, model-proposed, and derived values while preserving ?

Ranjan Sinha

Which interoperability fields are essential for exchanging typed evidence between agents?

Nilou Salehi

How would you test whether an executable reasoning structure contributes beyond structured prompting?

Furong Huang

What calibration evidence is needed before confidence safely controls model routing?

Raja Giryes

How should geometric uncertainty from a multimodal model propagate into downstream spatial computations?

Jon-Rav Shende, Bo Li, or John A McDermid

Which freshness, provenance, and attestation checks must pass before a verifier counts an answer?

Future of Agent Evaluation

Would a schema-matched control that changes only geometric correctness isolate grounding from prompt structure, or would you require another counterfactual?

Thirty-second follow-up structure

Connection: “Your point about [specific idea] connects directly to our Spatial Atlas work on [one contribution].”

Question: Ask the relevant question above.

Close: “May I send you the poster and paper link after the session?”
